About Us

Grand Pools was founded in 2021 with a vision to create exceptional swimming pools that transform outdoor spaces. With over 25 years of combined experience, our team specialises in designing and building pools that are both beautiful and built to last.

We focus on quality craftsmanship, innovation, and personalised service to bring every client's vision to life.

The Approach

Every commission moves through three stages. Each one led personally, each one designed to be clear, not lengthy. Restraint is the principle, even in the process.

(i) Site visit

Whether you have a team of 2 or 200, our shared team inboxes keep everyone on the same page and in the loop.

(ii) Design

A full set of drawings, materials, and finishes, presented in one considered conversation, not seven.

(iii) Build

One project at a time, supervised by the founder from excavation through commissioning.
